For those who want to prank their friend's phone, which turns out to be an Android phone using a Screen-Lock Pattern. This feature might be able to be bypassed using luck, but people's luck varies :P

I will share how to bypass this screen-lock, the tools needed:

1. Phone

2. Enough credit to make a call

The steps are as follows:

1. Prepare your phone along with its credit, the victim's phone is in a locked state.

2. Please call the victim's phone using your phone.

3. The victim's phone will automatically display the option to Answer or End the call.

4. Answer the call, and immediately press the menu button to display the Android desktop.

5. End your call, the victim's phone is no longer locked :)

\**Not all Android phones can be bypassed, the attempt I made succeeded on Android Samsung Galaxy Young, but failed on Smartfren Andro (Hisense E910)*
