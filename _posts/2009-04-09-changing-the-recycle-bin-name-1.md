Hmm... the method could be said to be quite easy by using Registry Editor....

- Select Start, click Run.
- Press 'Ctrl'+'F' or go directly from the find menu, and type Recycle Bin.
- Replace all Recycle Bin names with the name you want, for example Tong Sampah, or Tempat Sampah.
- Press F3 and replace every Recycle Bin name with your new name.
- Repeat step 4 until the Recycle Bin name is no longer found.
- Close the Registry Editor and on the Desktop press F5 or right-click and select Refresh.

Done, but to avoid fatal mistakes, you should back up the Registry first.
