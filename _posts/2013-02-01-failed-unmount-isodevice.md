Today I've decided to upgrade my mint 13 to 14 with the codename Nadia. I did it with a fresh install. When I was about to perform the installation, it turned out there was an error saying that /isodevice could not be unmounted.

The steps are quite easy

- Press the combination**CTRL + ALT + F1**

- type **sudo umount -l /isodevice.**

- Then return to the Installation menu and continue the install process by pressing **CTRL + ALT + F7 or F8**

**GOOD LUCK**
