Earlier today (21/7/2013), the account of the hacker who had attacked the ubuntuforums.org site (@Sputn1k_) stated that the forum's user data was safe, because it is not in his nature to misuse data obtained from the site he attacked.

**1.8 million user data** has been taken from the site, where the password encryption used uses a combination (md5(md5($pass).$salt). The standard built-in encryption of vBulletin, although not too strong, but to crack 1.8 million user data would take a very long time, as he revealed on his Twitter account.
