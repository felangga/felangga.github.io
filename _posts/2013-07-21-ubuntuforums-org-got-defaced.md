The favorite site where Ubuntu users discuss was defaced by a hacker with the Twitter ID [@Sputn1k_](https://twitter.com/Sputn1k_) yesterday on **20 July 2013** at **20.15** UTC. Unfortunately, it was reported that the *username, password,* and *email* in the Ubuntuforums database had been obtained by the hacker.

![Security breach at Ubuntu Forums compromised 1.82 million user s personal Information](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiL-A8pfxK_7XReeXPR0cng655wc3tmNQisuW2yCZ65HiYYAruOOsceUEczfeH0BN6wiouhY2MU1joze2p63I-gRQgTfIpg6gT00oRIkQM3HuQ4cOZ5Hav3BW3KRp8C9UKzZasMonQvy2k/s1600/Security+breach+at+Ubuntu+Forums+compromised+1.82+million+user%27s+personal+Information.png)

**1.8 million user data has been stolen** from the site, users who use the same password (**email**) are advised to immediately change your *password*.

Meanwhile, Ubuntu One, Launchpad, and Ubuntu/Canonical services remain safe. The Canonical team is still working hard on this, so let's wait for further developments.

![Security breach at Ubuntu Forums compromised](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjAKD9WwskIjX4a726Iqqzb_E1GOsnwJaAG9VMbBsV8Acngp1URG10P3q9l1yBCg67t4f6knr1F4xTl1QkvjrFEPIbpigw8B5ydeI66-zMRGyQb15RHtxwHDyGbJqVAsUVdbmsKSnh34WTF/s1600/Security+breach+at+Ubuntu+Forums+compromised.jpg)

The ubuntuforums.org site is still down for now after being defaced. **#PrayForUbuntuForums**
